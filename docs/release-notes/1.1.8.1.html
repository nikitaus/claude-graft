Usage for plans without a five-hour window

Ultra is spent against the week alone, and Graft used to refuse every usage answer that came without a five-hour figure. Those accounts showed "the usage service sent something unexpected" and fell back to figures recorded days or weeks earlier. They now show their week and their Fable share, live from Anthropic, with no five-hour bar, and the number in the menu bar is the week.

When a figure cannot be had live, the line under the account now says why: a login that has run out, one Anthropic refused, a rate limit, or no answer at all. Before, every one of those read as the service not answering.

Updates now come from this fork's own releases rather than the original project's feed.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, with macOS 13 or later supported.
