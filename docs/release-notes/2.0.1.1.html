Everything from Claude Graft 2.0.1

This release brings over the original project's 2.0 releases. A Claude can let its Claude Code draw on your other accounts when its own allowance runs low, with only the model's answers coming from them; sharing needs macOS 15. Three new panes reach claude.ai for every account you sign in to there: Artifacts, Projects and Search Chats. The window has a sidebar and fits without scrolling, every Claude and lent account has a colour of its own, the conversation on screen runs across the menu bar, and the Help menu leads to help, releases and problem reports.

The fixes that came with them are here too: recovered sessions are filed with your own browser permission setting, a login never passes through the shared web cache, a Deny at the keychain prompt is not followed by another, pinned chats sync again with current Claude versions, shortcuts are found after a rename and are created without stopping the window, and Claude's own updater stays hidden and signed out.

What stays as it was in this fork

Updates still come from this repository, signed with its own key. A figure that is not live still says why: a refused login, a rate limit and an answer that could not be read are named apart from the service not answering. The last live figure for each account is still kept across a restart, and the week is still read from the newer list of limits when the older window is missing. In the Help menu, What's New and Report a Problem open this repository.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, with macOS 13 or later supported.
