Everything in the original's 2.0.1

This release brings the fork up to the original project's 2.0.1, the largest release it has had. Your shortcuts, profiles and chats carry over as they are.

Share usage between your accounts

A Claude can now let its Claude Code draw on your other accounts when its own allowance runs low, without signing out of anything. Add another of your Claudes under Shared usage, or sign in to an account from Graft without making a shortcut for it. Only the model's answers come from the other accounts; your chats, artifacts and connectors stay with the Claude's own account, and the Chat tab is never shared. The menu bar shows every account a Claude draws on, rings the one answering in red, and runs the conversation on screen across it. The dropdown lets you choose which account answers each conversation. Sharing needs macOS 15.

Artifacts, projects and search across accounts

Three new panes reach claude.ai for every account you sign in to there. Artifacts lists them all together: open one, copy its link, make it private or public, rename, duplicate or delete it, and export it as Markdown, PDF, a Word document or its page source, or share it with a group you keep in Graft. Projects lists every account's projects, and Search Chats searches every account's chats and Claude Code sessions at once. Each account signs in once, with the code claude.ai emails you, in a window of Graft's; the sign-in is kept apart for that account and is never read by Graft.

A new window

The window has a proper sidebar, and every page fits at its smallest size without scrolling. Every Claude and every lent account gets a colour of its own. A new Claude starts with chats of its own, and sharing another Claude's chats is a choice you make on its page; the Claudes you already have keep reading what they read before. The new Help menu leads to the original project: its site, its releases, its issues and its author.

Pinned chats sync between profiles again with current versions of Claude, sessions from another Mac are left out of the sidebar, and Claude's updater stays hidden and signed out while it installs.

What this fork keeps

Accounts without a five-hour window, such as Ultra, are now shown the original's way: the row says the account has no five-hour limit in place of that bar, Start Session is left out for it, and the menu bar shows its week with a W in front, so W 94% means 94% of the week. Underneath, the fork still takes the week from the list of limits should the weekly window go missing, reads figures written with a fraction, and keeps the countdown for a reset written to the microsecond.

A login Anthropic refused and a rate limit still say so under the account, the last live figure still survives a restart, and updates still come from this fork's own releases.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, with macOS 13 or later supported.

Upgrading from 1.0.x

As with 1.1.0, shared profiles merge their chat histories on their next launch. Going back to a profile's own chats preserves what it brought, but copies already merged into the source stay there. If you need an untouched copy, back up your Claude profile folders in ~/Library/Application Support before upgrading.
